About Ephemeris

Ephemeris is a Universal app built specifically for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. It does not require Rosetta or any external dependencies. Logs are opened read-only — your original files are never modified.

Tip: PHD2 typically saves logs to ~/Documents/PHD2/ on Mac, or the equivalent Documents\PHD2\ folder if you copied them from a Windows acquisition machine. Either format is supported, including Windows CRLF line endings.
