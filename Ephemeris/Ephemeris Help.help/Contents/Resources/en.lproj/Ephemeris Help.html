Ephemeris Help

A native macOS reader for PHD2 guiding and calibration logs. Open a .txt log from PHD2 and review your night session-by-session, with RMS, drift, FFT, and visual cluster analysis.

Topics

  • Getting started Open a log, find your sessions, and read the summary.
  • The main window Sidebar, chart, inspector, and toolbar at a glance.
  • Reading the guide chart RA & Dec lines, units, axes, hovering, and overlays.
  • Statistics and drift RMS, peak, drift, and polar-alignment readouts.
  • Zoom and manual exclusions Drag to zoom or exclude noisy frame ranges from stats.
  • Combining sessions Stitch multiple guiding sections back into one night.
  • Frequency analysis Find periodic error and tracking issues with FFT.
  • Calibration view Read the dx/dy plot, leg rates, and orthogonality.
  • Exporting Share charts as PNG and stats or frames as CSV.
  • Keyboard shortcuts Every shortcut in one place.

About Ephemeris

Ephemeris is a Universal app built specifically for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. It does not require Rosetta or any external dependencies. Logs are opened read-only — your original files are never modified.

Tip: PHD2 typically saves logs to ~/Documents/PHD2/ (Mac) or the equivalent Documents\PHD2 folder if you copied them from a Windows acquisition machine. Either format is supported.
