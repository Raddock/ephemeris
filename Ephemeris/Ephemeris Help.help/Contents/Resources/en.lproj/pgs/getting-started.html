Getting started

Ephemeris launches into the Log Library — the app's home window. Every PHD2 .txt log you open is ingested there, building a per-rig, multi-night history; opening a log drills into an indexed single-night view.

First launch

On first launch the Library is empty. Get your logs in either way:

  1. Open a single log — choose File › Open (⌘O), or drag a .txt log onto the app icon in Finder or the Dock. The log opens in its own document window and is automatically ingested into the library.
  2. Import your whole archive — click Import logs… in the Library toolbar (or File › Import Folder of Logs…, ⇧⌘I) and pick a folder. Every PHD2_GuideLog_*.txt inside is scanned recursively, and rigs are created automatically from each log's PHD2 profile name. See Importing your log archive.

Logs are opened read-only. The app never writes to your original file.

Configure your rig

The PHD2 log only describes your guide camera — Ephemeris also needs the imaging side to judge results. Open Ephemeris › Rig Profiles… (⇧⌘,) and enter the imaging focal length, pixel size, binning, and any reducer. That computes your imaging pixel scale, which unlocks night verdicts, the star-shape predictor, and several observations. See Rig profiles.

Tip: The Library is where trends live — verdict-colored nights, the trend chart, and cross-night observations. See The Log Library for the full tour. Reopen it any time with ⇧⌘L.

Read a log's summary

Opening a log (or double-clicking a night in the Library) shows the document window with the Summary selected in the sidebar. It shows aggregate statistics across the whole log: total guiding time, frame counts, weighted RMS, plus pill-shaped Best and Worst session shortcuts in the header. The table below lists every guiding section in chronological order.

  • Click any row to jump to that session.
  • Each row's Total RMS column shows a thin quality bar scaled to the worst session in the log — outliers pop visually without having to read numbers across rows.
  • Best and worst rows are tagged with a green ★ or orange ⚠︎ chip in the leading column, and their RMS values are colour-tinted to match.
  • Click the Best or Worst capsule in the header to jump to those sessions directly.
  • Sort the table by clicking column headers.

Pick a session in the sidebar

The left sidebar lists every section with a small icon: a crosshair for guiding, a target for calibration. Each row shows the start time and duration. Click one to open it; click Summary to return to the overview.

Tip: A single PHD2 log often contains many short sections — meridian flips, cloud breaks, and dithering all start new sections. See Combining sessions for how to view a whole night as one chart.

Where PHD2 stores logs

  • macOS: ~/Documents/PHD2/
  • Windows: Documents\PHD2\ (copy across to Mac as-is — Windows line endings are handled)

Filenames typically look like PHD2_GuideLog_2026-04-24_205125.txt.

Staying up to date

Choose Ephemeris › Check for Updates… any time to check for a new version. Automatic checks happen only after you agree to them the first time the app asks.
