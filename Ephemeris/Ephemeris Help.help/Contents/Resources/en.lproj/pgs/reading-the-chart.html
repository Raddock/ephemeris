Reading the guide chart

The main chart plots the guide-star deviation over time. Two lines, one per axis, with optional overlays for corrections, diagnostics, and events.

Series

  • RA — right-ascension deviation. Positive values mean the star drifted east of the lock position.
  • Dec — declination deviation. Positive means north.

Toggle either series via Overlays › Series at the bottom of the chart.

Units

Switch between Pixels and Arc-sec with the segmented control on the chart-controls bar. The conversion uses the session's pixel scale, parsed from the log header. Arc-seconds are usually what you want when comparing nights — they're independent of camera and focal length.

Axes

Switch between RA / Dec and dx / dy:

  • RA / Dec — mount-frame deviation, with PHD2's atmospheric-refraction and orientation transforms applied. Use this for guiding-quality analysis.
  • dx / dy — raw camera-frame pixel offsets. Use this when investigating mechanical issues that present in the camera frame rather than along sky axes.

Y scale

The Y: Auto / ±0.5″ / ±1″ / ±2″ / ±5″ / ±10″ menu controls the vertical range. Auto pads 10% above and below the largest deviation in the visible range. A fixed range is useful for comparing nights or for spotting outliers without the chart auto-zooming to fit a single spike.

Hover and selection

Move the pointer across the chart to see the readout for the frame closest to the cursor: time, RA / Dec values, SNR. Click a frame to pin the readout (a vertical rule stays at that location even when you move the pointer). Click an empty area to dismiss it.

Overlays

Open Overlays at the bottom right of the chart:

  • Series: RA, Dec, Corrections (the bars showing pulse direction and magnitude per frame).
  • Diagnostics: Star mass and SNR sub-charts below the main chart. Useful for finding cloud passes and dropped frames.
  • Annotations: Events markers (settling, dither, frame drops), Limits (max guide-pulse), Grid, and the Scatter cluster overlay.

Scatter cluster overlay

A small RA/Dec scatter inset floats in the top-right of the chart, showing every visible frame as a yellow dot in a square XY plot. The cluster's shape tells you the wandering pattern at a glance — round means good seeing/balance, elongated means drift or backlash, sparse means few frames.

The scatter respects the current axis mode (RA/Dec vs dx/dy), units, visible time range, and any manual exclusions.

Tip: Use the chart's hover readout together with the inspector's Events list. Clicking an event jumps the chart's selection rule to that frame.
