Guide algorithms — when defaults are right

The algorithm turns each measured deviation into a correction pulse. PHD2 ships sensible defaults per axis, and for most mounts the right move is to leave them alone.

The algorithms

  • Hysteresis (RA default) — blends the current deviation with recent history, damping reaction to seeing while following real trends. Tunable via aggressiveness and hysteresis percentages.
  • ResistSwitch (Dec default) — resists changing direction until several consecutive frames agree, which is exactly what a backlash-prone Dec axis needs. Corrects steadily in one direction, reverses reluctantly.
  • Lowpass2 — fits a slope to recent history and corrects toward it, ignoring frame-to-frame noise almost entirely. Suited to mounts that track so well that only slow drift needs correcting.
  • ZFilter — a digital low-pass filter that lets you use short exposures while responding as if they were longer, smoothing seeing without adding correction lag.
  • Predictive PEC (PPEC) — models the mount's periodic error in real time (a Gaussian-process model) and corrects it predictively, before it appears, instead of reacting after. RA only in practice; needs a few worm cycles to train at the start of a session.

When the defaults are right

Almost always. Hysteresis-RA + ResistSwitch-Dec is the configuration the PHD2 developers tune for and the one virtually all troubleshooting advice assumes. Changing the algorithm to fix bad guiding usually treats a symptom whose real cause is polar alignment, balance, backlash, or seeing — check those first.

When to consider a change

  • PPEC — if RA is dominated by smooth, repeating periodic error from a worm-gear mount, PPEC can cut RA RMS substantially. The frequency analysis view shows whether a worm-period peak dominates your RA spectrum.
  • Lowpass2 — on premium mounts with absolute encoders (which have essentially no periodic error), where the only thing left to correct is slow drift.
Change one thing at a time: switching algorithms resets its parameters to defaults. Make the change, guide a full session, compare the numbers, then decide.
