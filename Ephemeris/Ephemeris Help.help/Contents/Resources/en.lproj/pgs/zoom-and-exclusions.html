Zoom and manual exclusions

Drag a range across the chart to zoom in, or to mark a span of frames as excluded from statistics.

Drag mode

The two-button toggle in the chart-controls bar selects what dragging does:

  • Zoom (magnifying glass) — the dragged range becomes the visible time range.
  • Exclude (dashed rectangle) — the dragged range is added to the manual exclusion list, and frames inside it are removed from RMS, peak, drift, and aggregate calculations.

Zoom workflow

  1. Set Drag mode to Zoom.
  2. Click and drag horizontally across the chart to define the range.
  3. Press ⌘0 or click Reset zoom in the toolbar to restore the full session.

Exclusion workflow

  1. Set Drag mode to Exclude.
  2. Drag across the noisy frames you want to ignore. The range appears as a translucent orange band.
  3. Statistics in the strip and inspector update immediately.
  4. Click Clear exclusions in the toolbar to remove all manual exclusions for the session.

What exclusions affect

  • Per-session RMS, peak, drift, and polar-alignment estimate.
  • The aggregate (frame-count-weighted) RMS in the Summary view.
  • The scatter-cluster overlay.
  • Frequency-analysis input.

What exclusions do not affect

  • The chart lines themselves — the data is still drawn so you can see what was excluded.
  • Exported frame CSV — the full per-frame data is exported. Excluded ranges only filter aggregated statistics CSVs.
  • The original log file. Exclusions are only stored in memory for the open session.
Tip: If your session contains a clear cloud pass with very low SNR, dragging an exclusion across that range gives you a more representative RMS for the rest of the night.
