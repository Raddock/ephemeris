The Log Library

The Library is the app's home window — every log you open or import is ingested into it, so each rig builds a multi-night history you can read at a glance.

Ephemeris launches into the Library. Reopen it any time with Window › Log Library (⇧⌘L).

Rig sidebar

The left column lists your rigs — one per PHD2 equipment profile, created automatically when a log is first opened or imported. Each row shows the rig's name (your display name if you set one, otherwise the PHD2 profile name), its imaging scale when configured, and its mount class. Select a rig to see its history; right-click (or press ⌫) to delete a rig along with its nights and observations.

Below the rigs, the Range section filters everything in the detail view: Week, Month, Year, All, or Custom range with From/To date pickers.

Header and metric cards

The header shows the rig name, its imaging scale capsule, and a share button (Share for help…) that generates a forum-ready summary — see When to ask for help and how. Three metric cards summarize the selected range:

  • Median RMS — the median of every night's median RMS, tagged with its verdict (Sub-pixel, At resolution, Over resolution).
  • Integration — total guided hours.
  • Sessions — total guiding sessions across the range.
Note: If the rig's imaging train isn't configured yet, a banner appears here instead of verdicts — set the imaging focal length, pixel size, and binning in Rig profiles to unlock them.

Hygiene strip

Three pills surface PHD2-canon maintenance signals, colored green / orange / red by staleness:

  • Calibration — how long since the most recent night's calibration.
  • Guiding Assistant — when GA last ran (red if never, or 90+ days).
  • Polar alignment — the last polar-error measurement from a GA run, in arc-minutes.

Each pill is a link to the relevant section of the PHD2 manual.

Trend chart

One point per observing night — nights are bucketed noon-to-noon, so a session restarted just after midnight stays with the previous evening's run. The Y axis is median RMS in arc-seconds; points are colored by verdict (green sub-pixel, amber at-resolution, coral over-resolution). A dashed orange line marks the rig's imaging pixel scale — nights below it should produce tight stars. Dashed blue vertical lines mark your annotations, so equipment changes line up visually with any change in the trend.

Cross-night observations

Between the chart and the table, the observations panel lists what the cross-night engine found across the selected range — calibration step-changes, baseline regressions, GA staleness, sub-quality discrepancies. See Reading observations for the card anatomy.

Recent Nights table

Every night in the selected range, most recent first. Columns:

Verdict dotColor-coded night quality — hover for the verdict name.
DateThe night's date, with a blue target chip (e.g. M 51) when Ephemeris recognized what you were imaging.
SessionsHow many guiding sessions the night contained. A blue speech-bubble badge follows when the night has annotations.
Open LogOpens the original PHD2 log in the single-night document window.
NoteAdds an annotation for this night.
IntegrationTotal guided minutes.
RMS ErrorThe night's median RMS in arc-seconds, tinted by verdict.
Star ShapePredicted star shape from the data — sharp, bloated, elongated, trailed, or mixed. Hover for the full reasoning, including the RMS-to-imaging-scale ratio. Your own sub-quality rating, when you've recorded one, feeds the same engine — see Annotations and rating your nights.
  • Double-click a row to open that night's original log in the document window.
  • Right-click for Open log… and Add annotation….
Tip: The Import logs… toolbar button ingests an entire folder of PHD2 logs in one pass — see Importing your log archive.
