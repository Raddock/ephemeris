Shortcuts and automation

Two App Shortcuts ship with Ephemeris. They appear automatically in Shortcuts.app, Spotlight, and Siri — no setup required.

The shortcuts

Open Most Recent LogOpens the most recent PHD2 guide log Ephemeris has ingested, straight into the document window. Say or type "Open the most recent Ephemeris log".
Show Recent TrendsOpens the Log Library pre-filtered to the past week. Say or type "Show Ephemeris trends".

Per-rig parameters

Both shortcuts take an optional Rig parameter. In Shortcuts.app, add the action to a workflow and pick the rig from the menu — Ephemeris supplies your rig list to the picker. With no rig set, the shortcut uses your most recent activity.

Where they run from

  • Spotlight — start typing the shortcut name and run it directly from results.
  • Shortcuts.app — combine with other actions; e.g. a "morning after" workflow that opens last night's log as soon as you sit down.
  • Siri — the spoken phrases above work once Ephemeris has been launched at least once.
Tip: Annotations you add in the Library are indexed by Spotlight too — searching for "OAG" or a rig name finds the matching notes alongside the shortcuts.
