Reading observations

Observations are Ephemeris's findings — single-night ones appear in the document window, cross-night ones in the Library. Every card states its evidence and where its advice comes from.

Anatomy of a card

Click anywhere on a card to expand it. From top to bottom:

  • Severity bar — the colored bar on the card's leading edge (and the matching dot beside the severity label).
  • Title — a specific statement, with the measured number in it where possible.
  • Severity · source badge — the metadata row beneath the title.
  • Summary — what was seen and why it matters (clamped to three lines until expanded).
  • Evidence — the measured values the finding rests on, with thresholds where relevant.
  • Possible contributors — candidate causes, when the data can't single one out.
  • Suggested response — the concrete next step, usually naming the PHD2 tool to reach for.
  • PHD2 tools referenced / Learn more — links to the PHD2 manual and to related Ephemeris help topics.

Severity tiers

Alert (red)Strong evidence of a genuine problem. Rare by design.
Pattern (orange)A named, observed pattern that needs your judgment.
Equipment (yellow)Optical-train or mechanical category.
Hygiene (blue)PHD2 tool freshness or configuration — calibration age, GA staleness.
Suggestion (teal)Imaging-side decisions.
Coaching (gray)Process improvements within normal operation.

Source-authority badges

Every observation declares where its advice comes from, so you can weigh it accordingly:

PHD2 docsCited directly from PHD2's manual or Best Practices document.
PHD2 measuredPHD2 itself measured this — Guiding Assistant or Calibration Assistant output, quoted verbatim. Supersedes anything Ephemeris computes in parallel.
PHD2 alertBehavior PHD2 documents as an alert — calibration sanity checks, max-duration limits, star-lost codes.
community consensusNot in the PHD2 docs but widely accepted — harmonic-mount tuning, OAG-vs-guide-scope wisdom.
Ephemeris heuristicComputed from your corpus by Ephemeris itself — rig-relative baselines, threshold tiers.
Note: Heuristic and community-consensus observations deliberately use softened language ("worth checking" rather than "is wrong"). That's intentional — only PHD2's own measurements and documentation get the assertive register.

Triage order

  1. Start with Alert-severity cards — they carry the strongest evidence.
  2. Prefer PHD2 measured values over anything computed: if the Guiding Assistant has spoken, its min-move and backlash numbers win.
  3. Hygiene items (stale calibration, GA never run) are cheap to clear and often resolve downstream patterns — handle them before chasing equipment theories.

Making an observation go away

Observations aren't to-dos you check off — they recompute from the data, and clear when the underlying condition clears. Two ways to silence one deliberately:

  • Annotate the night. If a step-change observation fired because you did change something (new OAG, camera rotation, recalibration), add an annotation with the matching category. The engine attributes the change to your note and stops flagging it within a few nights of the event. See Annotations and rating your nights.
  • Fix the input. Several findings hinge on the rig profile — a wrong guide focal length or missing imaging scale can produce observations that vanish once the profile is corrected.
