Frequency analysis

A periodogram of the RA or Dec deviation that surfaces dominant periods — useful for finding worm gear errors, mount resonances, or seeing-driven oscillations.

Open the analysis

With a guiding session selected, click Frequency analysis in the toolbar (⌘F). A modal sheet opens with the chart and controls.

The session must contain at least 32 included frames. Shorter sessions don't produce a meaningful spectrum.

Reading the chart

X axis is period in seconds, on a log scale — longer periods on the right, shorter on the left. Y axis is the magnitude of that frequency component (arbitrary units; relative comparisons are meaningful, absolute values are not).

The dominant period is annotated with a vertical rule and a label showing its period in seconds.

Axis picker

Choose RA or Dec. RA usually shows worm-gear periodic error; Dec is dominated by drift and seeing.

Drift correct

Toggle on to subtract a linear trend before the FFT. Without this, low-frequency leakage from a slow drift dominates the spectrum and hides genuine periodic signals. Leave it on as the default.

Pipeline (technical)

  1. Filter to included frames only.
  2. Resample to a uniform time grid (cubic interpolation).
  3. Optionally subtract the linear-regression trend.
  4. Subtract mean.
  5. Apply a Hann window (reduces spectral leakage at the boundaries).
  6. Zero-pad to the next power of two.
  7. Real FFT via Accelerate's vDSP_fft_zripD.
  8. Take magnitudes; convert bin index to period using period = N · dt / i.
Tip: A persistent spike near 480 s on RA is a classic worm-gear signature for many mounts. Comparing that across nights tells you whether your PEC training is still good.
