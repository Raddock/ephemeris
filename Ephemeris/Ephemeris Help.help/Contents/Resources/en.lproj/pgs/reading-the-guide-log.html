Reading your guide log

PHD2 records everything it does to a plain-text log. Ephemeris is built entirely on that file — here is what's in it and how the chart maps to it.

What PHD2 writes

Each time PHD2 runs it creates a file named like PHD2_GuideLog_2026-06-09_213045.txt (on the Mac, in ~/Documents/PHD2/). Inside is an ordered stream of independent sections:

  • Guiding sections — begin with Guiding Begins at …, then a header (equipment, guide algorithms, pixel scale, target RA / Dec), then one table row per guide exposure until Guiding Ends. Every section becomes a session in Ephemeris's sidebar.
  • Calibration sections — begin with Calibration Begins at … and record each calibration step. These feed the Calibration view.
  • INFO events — lines interleaved with the frame table: dithers, settling state changes, guiding-parameter changes, lock-position moves, star-lost messages. Ephemeris shows these as event markers on the chart and in the inspector's Events card.

The frame table

Each guide exposure is one row. The columns Ephemeris uses most:

TimeSeconds since the session started — the chart's X axis.
dx, dyStar offset from the lock position, in camera-frame pixels. Shown when the axis mode is dx / dy.
RARawDistance, DECRawDistanceThe same offset decomposed onto the sky axes — the RA and Dec lines on the chart. "Raw" means before the guide algorithm filters it.
RAGuideDistance, DECGuideDistanceThe offset after the guide algorithm (hysteresis, min-move, etc.) — what PHD2 actually computed a pulse from.
RADuration / RADirection
DECDuration / DECDirection		The correction pulse in milliseconds, plus a direction letter. Ephemeris stores these signed — East and North positive, West and South negative — so the Corrections overlay draws bars above or below zero matching pulse direction.
StarMass, SNRBrightness and signal-to-noise of the guide star — the Star mass and SNR diagnostic sub-charts.
ErrorCodeNon-zero (other than "saturated") means the frame failed — star lost, low SNR — and is excluded from statistics automatically.

Camera axes vs sky axes

The guide camera measures dx / dy in its own frame; PHD2's calibration determines the rotation that maps those onto RA and Dec:

That is why the chart offers both axis modes: RA / Dec for guiding quality, dx / dy for mechanical issues that live in the camera frame. See Reading the guide chart.

RMS, drift, settling, exclusions

  • RMS RA / RMS Dec — the standard deviation of each axis's raw distance across included frames; Total RMS is the geometric sum √(RA² + Dec²). Details in Statistics & drift.
  • Drift — the slow linear trend under the noise, in ″/min. Dec drift is the classic polar-alignment signature.
  • Settling — after a dither, PHD2 waits for the star to stay within a tolerance before imaging resumes. Settling periods are drawn as translucent bands on the chart — orange when settling failed.
  • Excluded frames — rows with an error code are skipped in every statistic, as are ranges you exclude by hand (Zoom & exclusions). The frame count X / Y means X included of Y total.
Tip: The guide log is documented authoritatively by the PHD2 project itself — see the PHD2 manual for what each setting that appears in the section header means.
Remember: the log records the guide camera only. Problems that never reach the guide sensor — flexure, mirror flop, focus drift — leave no trace here. See What the guide log can't tell you.

Where this shows up in Ephemeris

Everything described above is the document window: the sidebar lists each guiding and calibration section, the chart plots the frame table, the Star mass / SNR sub-charts plot the diagnostics columns, and the inspector's Events card lists the INFO lines. The statistics cards and the Library's per-night numbers are all computed from these same rows.
